Independent Research Lab

Biology, materials, and machine intelligence — one lab, one loop.

Skora is a small, independent R&D lab working at the intersection of synthetic biology, advanced materials, and applied AI — designing therapeutic peptides, discovering next-generation alloys and polymers, and building the generative models that connect the two, closing the loop between hypothesis and working molecule faster than any one discipline can alone.

Why All Three

Three disciplines running as one loop, not three teams in a row.

Most research happens in a chain: chemists hand results to materials scientists, who eventually hand results to a software team bolted on at the end. Skora runs biology, materials science, and AI as one continuous loop — models propose a peptide sequence or an alloy composition, the lab synthesizes it and runs the assay or the stress test, and every result trains the next round of models.

Focus Areas

What we're actually working on

Three specimens, one bench. Each area is worked independently and fed back into the other two.

SPECIMEN 01

Peptide & Protein Engineering

Designing therapeutic peptides, macrocycles, and protein binders computationally, then validating binding affinity and stability on the bench.

  • ModalitiesTherapeutic peptides, cyclic peptides/macrocycles, protein binders
  • MethodsSolid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS), phage & mRNA display, protein language models
SPECIMEN 02

Advanced Materials Discovery

Searching high-entropy alloy, polymer, and biomaterial composition space with active learning instead of trial and error — then synthesizing and characterizing what the models find.

  • ModalitiesHigh-entropy alloys, polymers & composites, biomaterial scaffolds
  • MethodsDFT simulation, active-learning search, XRD & mechanical characterization
SPECIMEN 03

Models for Molecular & Materials Design

Building the generative and predictive models that connect the other two — trained on our own assay and characterization data, and judged by what they get right in the lab.

  • ModalitiesGenerative sequence & structure models, retrosynthesis prediction
  • MethodsDiffusion & language models, active-learning loops, virtual screening
Approach

How a candidate moves through the lab

The same five phases, whether it's a molecule, a material, or a model.

Phase 01

Hypothesize

Start from a real constraint — a property, a function, a failure mode worth solving.

Phase 02

Model

Generate and rank candidates computationally before spending any bench time.

Phase 03

Synthesize

Build the highest-value candidates in the lab, not the entire search space.

Phase 04

Validate

Test against the original constraint — not a proxy metric that's easier to hit.

Phase 05

Iterate

Every result — good or bad — retrains the model for the next round.

About Skora

Small, independent, and early — on purpose.

We're not trying to be a biotech company, a materials company, or an AI company. We're trying to be the lab that treats those as one problem — because the biggest discoveries usually sit in the gap between disciplines, not inside one of them.

Skora is an early-stage, independent research lab. We'd rather spend the time getting the loop right than run a slow version of it at scale. If that's a problem you're thinking about too — as a researcher, an institution, or a partner — we want to hear from you.
