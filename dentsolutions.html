What We Do

Complete Dent Repair Solutions

From minor dings to major hail damage, we restore your vehicle to its original condition — no paint required in most cases.

Paintless Dent Repair

Our signature service uses specialized tools to gently massage dents out from behind the panel — preserving your factory paint finish and saving you thousands over traditional bodywork.

Hail Damage Repair

Storm left a mess on your hood or roof? We specialize in hail damage restoration using PDR techniques that restore every panel to pre-storm condition — fast and cost-effectively.

Bumper Repair & Refinishing

Scratched or cracked bumpers repaired and refinished to match your vehicle's exact color. Avoid costly dealer replacements with our precision repair approach.

Minor Paint Repair

Small chips, scratches, and scuffs touched up with color-matched paint. Protect your vehicle from rust and keep it looking sharp between major services.

Fully Mobile Service

No need to drop your car off or arrange a loaner. We come to your home, office, or anywhere convenient — getting you back on the road with zero disruption to your day.

Free Estimates

Not sure what your repair will cost? We provide honest, no-pressure written estimates before any work begins. Know exactly what you're paying — always.
